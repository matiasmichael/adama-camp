ADAMA is a camp where people are welcomed as they are, grounded by the earth, and gathered around the fire.

What ADAMA holds

The Fire

Global Warming Fire Pit

Our central gathering point. Open to the whole camp, tended around the clock. Come sit, talk, or watch the embers rise.

The Temple

A space for becoming

A quiet place for reflection and transformation. No performance required. Propose programming or simply arrive.

The Welcome

Arrive as you are

No costumes needed, no auditions. ADAMA is fifteen people holding space for anyone who walks in from the dust.

The Ground

Bare feet on ancient dust

We are part of Entheos this year, in service of the whole playa. The earth here has seen everything. You are safe on it.

Welcome

You are received as you are. No performance required.

Ground

Earthy, physical, embodied. Bare feet on ancient dust.

Fire

The gathering point. Warmth that is always burning.

Becoming

Camp essentials

When

August 30 – September 7, 2026. Gates open Sunday. The fire is lit from the first night.

Where

Within Entheos, Black Rock City. Look for the fire pit and the handmade ring-and-flame mark.

Who

Fifteen campers from many places. Some build, some run, some strike. All tend the fire.

What to bring

Yourself. Warm layers for night. An open hand. The dust provides the rest.

Want to help with the Global Warming Fire Pit or propose Temple programming? Find Michael on playa. He coordinates the people, the ideas, and the schedule.
