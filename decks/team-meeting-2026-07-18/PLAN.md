# ADAMA Team Meeting Deck Plan

Date: July 18, 2026
Audience: First full ADAMA camper group
Format: 16-slide, 16:9 HTML presentation with verified PDF export
Presenter: Michael Matias

## Meeting outcome

Everyone leaves excited and calm, understands the journey and ADAMA operating model, has space to ask questions, and commits to completing the preference form within 48 hours. No roles are assigned live.

## Story arc

1. Meaning: the collective enables individual journeys.
2. Orientation: where ADAMA lives and how we reach Playa.
3. Operating promise: responsibility before the Burn creates freedom during it.
4. Commitment: every camper joins Building, Running, or Striking and tends the fire.
5. Discussion: surface constraints and questions without assigning roles live.
6. Action: one response per camper within 48 hours.

## Source boundaries

- Exact strike timing.

- Individual ticket and payment tails.
- Named role assignments.

## Slide contract

1. **Cover**
   - Job: welcome the first full ADAMA group into the meeting.
   - Content: ADAMA lockup, 42 Days to the Playa, July 18, 2026.
   - Feel: dark, cinematic, firelit.

2. **Why we gather**
   - Job: establish that the collective exists to enable individual journeys.
   - Content: Michael's eighth-Burn perspective and the camp promise.
   - Feel: intimate, spacious.

3. **Participation changes the Burn**
   - Job: contrast spectator posture with contribution.
   - Content: choose your day, return to a home we built together.
   - Feel: direct, energizing.

4. **Our home inside Entheos**
   - Job: locate ADAMA without overclaiming map detail.
   - Content: 9:15 and Esplanade shorthand plus source-map precision.
   - Visual: corrected Entheos placement map.

5. **The Big C**
   - Job: make the camp footprint tangible.
   - Content: 69 x 105 ft, six RVs, two tents, courtyard opening toward center camp.
   - Locked slots: RV1 Lian + Peretz; RV2 Gilad + Ronny + Yarden; RV3 Michael + Hanna + Yossi; RV4 Shay + Ira + Alan + Sean; RV5 Sagi + Oren; RV6 Or + Amir.
   - Locked tents: Shahar in the left internal marker; Eleanor + Nir in the shared Kodiak 10x10 in the right internal marker.
   - Visual: a presentation redraw using the exact approved v9 coordinates, with the stale Shiftpod label removed.

6. **The journey to Playa**
   - Job: orient everyone to the travel sequence.
   - Content: Friday dinner; Saturday Building path by noon; two El Monte RVs at Dublin from 1 to 4 PM; four other RV groups with separate pickup plans; dynamic Reno-or-direct route; Burn and return window.
   - Visual: parallel Saturday tracks, not a false sequential timeline.
   - Feel: clear trail, low cognitive load.

7. **Freedom needs structure**
   - Job: introduce the governing operating model.
   - Content: Building, Running, Striking, plus universal fire shift.
   - Feel: decisive and calm.

8. **Building**
   - Job: explain the early-arrival commitment.
   - Content: minimum two people and one RV by noon Saturday; setup responsibilities.
   - Boundary: roster not assigned live.

9. **Running**
   - Job: name the families of in-Burn responsibility.
   - Content: technical Fire Pit operations, Temple, camp point, RV service coordination.
   - Boundary: these are additional Running responsibilities, separate from the universal Fire Shift.
   - Boundary: mechanics are finalized after forms and safety constraints.

10. **Everyone tends the fire**
    - Job: make the shared cultural responsibility explicit.
    - Content: host the Playa, tend the space, leave it ready for the next shift.
    - Boundary: shift duration and staffing are not final.

11. **Striking**
    - Job: connect departure to responsibility and Leave No Trace.
    - Content: pack, sweep, MOOP check, Entheos handoff.
    - Boundary: exact timing is finalized later.

12. **A responsibility is real when...**
    - Job: define the camp's reliability standard.
    - Content: owner, time, definition of done, handoff or backup.
    - Feel: plain, memorable.

13. **What the form asks**
    - Job: reduce friction before people scan.
    - Content: group preferences, impossible groups, role interests, partner, mobility or health constraints, fire-shift constraints.

14. **What happens next**
    - Job: explain post-form assignment logic.
    - Content: synthesize constraints, assign roles, confirm handoffs, publish one clear plan.

15. **Questions**
    - Job: create room for live dialogue before the action.
    - Prompt: What do you need to know, challenge, or name before this can work?
    - Feel: quiet, open.

16. **Choose your role**
    - Job: secure the commitment.
    - Content: QR code, public form URL, one response per camper, Monday deadline, next-step summary.
    - Feel: warm, decisive, final.
